Stance

It started at factory height.

Sensible gap over the tyres and soft springs. Fine for a daily. Not the car in the photos.

Then it came down.

Blueline coilovers, set stiff and as low as it can go while still working on a real road. The centre of gravity drops with it.

Then it was dialled in.

A full alignment after the drop, with conservative street toe. Low, stiff and still a car you can drive every day.

Exhaust

Three inches.
Nothing in the way.

A full 3″ system from the turbo back, split in two and finished with polished stainless twin tips on each side of the diffuser. Four outlets in total.

Catalytic converterRemoved. The pipe runs straight through.
Diesel particulate filterRemoved along with its back-pressure.
Rear mufflerRemoved. The Y-split feeds the tips directly.
